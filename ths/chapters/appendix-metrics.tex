\chapter{Metrics Reference}
\label{app:metrics}

This appendix documents the Prometheus metrics exported by each sequencer, the PromQL queries used in the dashboard panels, and the raw metric output recorded during a live evaluation run.

\section{Exported Metrics}

Each sequencer exposes its metrics on a dedicated port: ZK on port~9100, Optimistic on port~9101. A single Prometheus instance scrapes both every five seconds. ZK metrics carry the \texttt{l2\_} prefix; Optimistic metrics carry \texttt{opt\_l2\_}.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{>{\ttfamily}l|l|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{5.5cm}}
\hline
\normalfont\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline
l2\_transactions\_total & Counter & Cumulative ZK L2 transactions accepted \\
l2\_batches\_total & Counter & Cumulative ZK batches submitted to L1 \\
l2\_tps & Gauge & ZK transactions per second (2~s window) \\
l2\_pending\_transactions & Gauge & ZK transactions awaiting next batch \\
l2\_finality\_time\_seconds & Histogram & L1 submission latency per ZK batch \\
l2\_proof\_generation\_seconds & Histogram & Simulated proof computation time \\
l2\_batch\_gas\_used & Gauge & EVM gas used by last ZK batch submission \\
\hline
opt\_l2\_transactions\_total & Counter & Cumulative Optimistic L2 transactions \\
opt\_l2\_batches\_total & Counter & Cumulative Optimistic batches submitted \\
opt\_l2\_tps & Gauge & Optimistic transactions per second \\
opt\_l2\_pending\_transactions & Gauge & Optimistic transactions awaiting next batch \\
opt\_l2\_finality\_time\_seconds & Histogram & L1 submission latency per Optimistic batch \\
opt\_l2\_batch\_processing\_seconds & Histogram & Batch assembly and hashing time \\
opt\_l2\_batch\_gas\_used & Gauge & EVM gas used by last Optimistic submission \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\label{tab:metrics-list}
\end{table}

\section{PromQL Queries Used in the Dashboard}

\begin{listing}[H]
    \begin{minted}[breaklines=true, breakanywhere=true, fontsize=\footnotesize]{text}
# Snapshot counter values
l2_transactions_total
opt_l2_transactions_total
l2_batches_total
opt_l2_batches_total

# Gas per batch (last observed value — gauge)
l2_batch_gas_used
opt_l2_batch_gas_used

# L1 submission latency P95 (rolling 1-minute window)
histogram_quantile(0.95,
  rate(l2_finality_time_seconds_bucket[1m]))
histogram_quantile(0.95,
  rate(opt_l2_finality_time_seconds_bucket[1m]))

# Proof generation overhead (ZK only, in milliseconds)
histogram_quantile(0.95,
  rate(l2_proof_generation_seconds_bucket[1m])) * 1000

# Batch assembly overhead (Optimistic, in milliseconds)
histogram_quantile(0.95,
  rate(opt_l2_batch_processing_seconds_bucket[1m])) * 1000

# Transaction throughput rate (rolling 1-minute)
rate(l2_transactions_total[1m])
rate(opt_l2_transactions_total[1m])
    \end{minted}
    \caption{PromQL queries for Grafana panels and direct Prometheus exploration at \texttt{localhost:9090}}
    \label{lst:promql-queries}
\end{listing}

\textbf{Important:} Histogram metric base names (e.g.\ \texttt{l2\_proof\_generation\_seconds}) are not directly queryable in Prometheus. Always query the sub-metrics: \texttt{\_bucket}, \texttt{\_count}, or \texttt{\_sum}.
